Y2K Profile — Jade Valentine demo (2007)

These 15 images are original AI-generated fictional portraits and scenes created
for the Y2K Profile demo. They do not depict real users or document real events.
They may be used with Y2K Profile and profiles exported from it.

9 portraits: Jade, Roxy, Ash, Mia, Ben, Lex, Hana, Jamie and Zoe.
6 album photographs: mirror, gig-night, headphones, mix-cds, train-home, weekend.
The mix-cds image also supplies the demo's Google+ cover.

Made with the built-in image generation tool. Original source PNGs and complete
prompts/reference relationships are retained in references/demo-2007/ in the source
project. Delivered images are WebP, resized without additional image manipulation.

The official YouTube video is embedded from its provider; it is not part of this
image collection. The original demo audio has its own notice in ../media/README.txt.
